# Evidence Strength Definitions

## STRONG
Biopsy-paired or large-RCT evidence, dose-response established.

## MODERATE
Meta-analysis or single well-powered RCT.

## WEAK / INCONCLUSIVE
Evidence exists but the effect is not statistically significant,
or confidence intervals cross zero.

## INSUFFICIENT
No adequate evidence for the specific recipe. The ranking engine
excludes or flags it rather than guessing.
